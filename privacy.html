Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Soforth LLC (“Soforth”, “we”) is a creative studio in Colorado. This policy explains what we collect when you use asksoforth.com, apply for a Strategy Session or work with us, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Your application: your name, brand name, email, website, Instagram handle and your answers about your business.
  • Engagement details: signed agreements, scheduling details, messages you send us, and the files and information you share in your private workspace.
  • Payments: handled by Square. We see that a payment was made and its amount; we never see or store your card number.
  • Spam protection: the application form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to check that a person, not a bot, is applying. Cloudflare processes basic technical data to do this.

This site does not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers or tracking pixels. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your browser’s IP address to deliver them.

How we use it

  • To review your application and reply to you.
  • To send your agreement, take your reservation and schedule your session.
  • To deliver the work you hire us for, inside your private workspace.
  • To keep records we are required to keep, such as signed agreements and payments.

We collect only what we need for these purposes. We don’t sell your information or share it for advertising.

Who helps us

We use a small number of service providers to run Soforth: Supabase (secure database and file storage), Vercel (hosting), Resend (email), Square (payments), Calendly (scheduling your session), Cloudflare (spam protection) and Anthropic (the AI model inside your workspace, used under terms that do not allow it to train on your data). Each receives only what it needs to do its job.

How long we keep it

Applications that don’t go ahead are deleted within 12 months. Client records, agreements and payment records are kept for as long as the law requires, usually seven years. Ask us any time to delete what we don’t need to keep.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or ask us to stop emailing you. Write to hello@asksoforth.com and we’ll reply within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above. Significant changes will be emailed to current clients.
